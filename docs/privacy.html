Privacy policy

This privacy policy describes how Gymvero (“we”, “us”) handles information in the Gymvero gym-owner mobile app available on the App Store and Google Play.

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · App: Gymvero

Who this app is for

Gymvero is a business tool for gym and studio owners to manage members, memberships, attendance, payments/dues, and their Gymvero subscription plan preference.

Information we process

Current store-review build

Sign-in uses review credentials. Session and gym operational sample data (members, plans, attendance, payments, profile edits) are stored on your device. We do not send this data to Gymvero servers in this version.

When cloud accounts launch, we may process account identifiers (such as email or phone), gym profile details, and operational data you enter to provide the service. We will not sell member lists.

How we use information

  • To provide and improve the Gymvero owner app
  • To authenticate you and keep your session
  • To support account deletion and customer requests
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers only as needed to operate the product (for example hosting or analytics after those features are enabled), or when required by law.

Data retention

On-device review data remains until you clear app data, sign out, or delete your account in the app. After cloud launch, we retain account data while your account is active and delete it when you request deletion, except where we must retain limited records for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your Gymvero owner account in the app: More → Delete account (or Gym profile → Delete account). Type DELETE to confirm. This is a permanent deletion of the account and associated personal data available to Gymvero — not a temporary deactivation.

You can also request deletion on the web: Account deletion page, or email privacy@gymvero.app.

Children

Gymvero is intended for business owners and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region).
