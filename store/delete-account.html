Delete your account

This page is the external account-deletion resource for the Gymvero gym owner app on Google Play and the App Store.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Gymvero and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Gym profile → Delete account, or More → Delete account.
  3. Confirm by typing DELETE, then choose Delete my account.

This permanently deletes your owner account and associated app data available to Gymvero (not a temporary deactivation).

Request by email

Email privacy@gymvero.app from the address on your Gymvero account. Include:

  • Subject: Gymvero account deletion request
  • Your registered email or phone
  • Gym / business name (if applicable)

We will confirm the request and delete the account and associated personal data, except where retention is required by law (described in our privacy policy).
